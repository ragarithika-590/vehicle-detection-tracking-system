1. YOLOv8 Detection

Utilizes pre-trained yolov8s.pt neural network architecture to perform high-speed bounding box estimation on road vehicle classes: Car (ID:2), Motorcycle (ID:3), Bus (ID:5), Truck (ID:7).

2. ByteTrack Object Tracking

Integrates ByteTrack multi-object tracking to associate detections across consecutive frames, maintaining unique track IDs even during occlusion, camera jitter, or temporary visual obstacles.

3. Duplicate Prevention

Ensures strict mathematical accuracy by indexing (vehicle_class, track_id) pairs per video session. A vehicle passing through 300 frames is counted exactly ONCE, not 300 times.
